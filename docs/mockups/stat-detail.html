Opening Candle Continuation

After the first N-minute candle of the NY session, how often does the session close in the same direction?

Definition

After the first 15-minute candle of the NY session, how often does the session close in the same direction? A green opening candle is compared against a green session close, a red opening candle against a red close.

Methodology — every probability is measured against a random baseline computed on the same confirmed sample. Pending (unresolved) sessions are excluded from both numerator and denominator. The edge column is probability minus baseline; positive means the condition beats chance.
